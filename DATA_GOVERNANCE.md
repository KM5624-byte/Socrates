# Data governance

Public releases require a verified source, permitted reuse, a coverage statement, reproducible normalization and a validation report. User identity and individual applicant and employee records are outside the pilot data model.

Preserve source attributes and documents where reuse permits. Ingest aggregate records only. Register licensing restrictions before public reuse. Corrections and revised series create versioned releases. A manifest records the source checksum and transformation, and a citation records the exact release involved. Retain the older record when superseded and document review decisions once the workflow exists.

Published research requires methods review. Use historical disclosure observations only when material evidence supports the capture date and attribute. Record inaccessible, not reviewed, and absent-in-document separately.
